# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

**Sprint:** Operations Sprint — Vendor Management (T6)
**Owner:** Operations Team
**Standards:** ISO 9001:2015 | SCOR Model | ISO 28000 | Procurement Best Practices
**Status:** Active
**Review Cadence:** Monthly

---

# Purpose

This document establishes the Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) used to measure the effectiveness of Vynues' Vendor Management process. These KPIs evaluate vendor onboarding, vendor performance, risk management, and service quality to support informed procurement decisions and continuous operational improvement.

---

# Objectives

The objectives of these KPIs are to:

- Measure vendor onboarding efficiency.
- Evaluate vendor performance using standardized metrics.
- Monitor vendor compliance with procurement contracts and Service Level Agreements (SLAs).
- Identify vendor-related risks before they impact operations.
- Support data-driven procurement decisions.
- Promote continuous improvement in vendor management.

---

# Vendor Onboarding KPIs

## Vendor Onboarding Cycle Time

**Definition**

Average number of business days required to complete the vendor onboarding process.

**Formula**

Total Onboarding Days ÷ Number of Vendors Onboarded

**Target**

≤ 5 Business Days

**Owner**

Operations Team

**Review Frequency**

Monthly

---

## Vendor Documentation Completion Rate

**Definition**

Percentage of vendors that submit all required documentation during onboarding.

**Formula**

(Vendors with Complete Documentation ÷ Total Vendors Onboarded) × 100

**Target**

100%

**Owner**

Operations Team

**Review Frequency**

Monthly

---

## Vendor Approval Rate

**Definition**

Percentage of vendors successfully approved after completing the onboarding process.

**Formula**

(Approved Vendors ÷ Total Vendor Applications) × 100

**Target**

≥ 95%

**Owner**

Procurement Team

**Review Frequency**

Quarterly

---

# Vendor Performance KPIs

## Vendor Performance Score

**Definition**

Average score received from the Vendor Performance Scorecard.

**Formula**

Total Vendor Scores ÷ Number of Evaluations

**Target**

≥ 4.5 / 5

**Owner**

Vendor Manager

**Review Frequency**

Quarterly

---

## Vendor On-Time Delivery Rate

**Definition**

Percentage of vendor deliveries completed by the agreed delivery date.

**Formula**

(On-Time Deliveries ÷ Total Deliveries) × 100

**Target**

≥ 95%

**Owner**

Vendor Manager

**Review Frequency**

Monthly

---

## SLA Compliance Rate

**Definition**

Percentage of vendor engagements meeting all Service Level Agreement requirements.

**Formula**

(SLA-Compliant Engagements ÷ Total Engagements) × 100

**Target**

≥ 95%

**Owner**

Vendor Manager

**Review Frequency**

Monthly

---

## Documentation Accuracy

**Definition**

Percentage of vendor records and procurement documentation completed accurately without corrections.

**Formula**

(Accurate Documents ÷ Total Documents Reviewed) × 100

**Target**

≥ 99%

**Owner**

Operations Team

**Review Frequency**

Monthly

---

# Vendor Risk KPIs

## High-Risk Vendor Percentage

**Definition**

Percentage of vendors classified as Tier 3 (High Risk).

**Formula**

(Tier 3 Vendors ÷ Total Vendors) × 100

**Target**

≤ 10%

**Owner**

Operations Team

**Review Frequency**

Quarterly

---

## Corrective Action Completion Rate

**Definition**

Percentage of corrective actions completed by the required deadline.

**Formula**

(Corrective Actions Completed ÷ Total Corrective Actions) × 100

**Target**

100%

**Owner**

Vendor Manager

**Review Frequency**

Monthly

---

# Continuous Improvement KPIs

## Vendor Performance Improvement

**Definition**

Percentage increase in average vendor performance scores over time.

**Formula**

((Current Performance Score − Previous Performance Score) ÷ Previous Performance Score) × 100

**Target**

Positive Improvement Each Review Period

**Owner**

Operations Team

**Review Frequency**

Quarterly

---

## Vendor Retention Rate

**Definition**

Percentage of approved vendors that continue working with Vynues over time.

**Formula**

(Returning Approved Vendors ÷ Total Approved Vendors) × 100

**Target**

≥ 90%

**Owner**

Procurement Team

**Review Frequency**

Annually
