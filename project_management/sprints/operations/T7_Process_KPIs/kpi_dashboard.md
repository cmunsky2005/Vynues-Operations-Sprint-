# Purpose

The Vendor Management KPI Dashboard provides a centralized view of vendor management performance across onboarding, performance evaluation, service delivery, and risk management. The dashboard enables management to monitor key metrics, identify performance trends, and make informed procurement decisions.

# Performance Status Legend

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| 🟢 On Target | KPI meets or exceeds the established target. |
| 🟡 Needs Attention | KPI is slightly below target and should be monitored. |
| 🔴 Action Required | KPI is significantly below target and requires immediate corrective action. |

# Dashboard Review Process

## Monthly Review

The Operations Team reviews all vendor management KPIs to:

- Monitor vendor onboarding performance.
- Evaluate vendor service quality.
- Review SLA compliance.
- Identify documentation issues.
- Track corrective actions.

---

## Quarterly Review

Management reviews long-term vendor performance trends to:

- Evaluate vendor relationships.
- Assess procurement effectiveness.
- Review high-risk vendors.
- Determine opportunities for process improvements.

---

## Annual Review

Leadership conducts a comprehensive evaluation to:

- Compare annual vendor performance against organizational goals.
- Reassess KPI targets.
- Review vendor retention and strategic partnerships.
- Update vendor management policies and procedures.

---

# Corrective Action Triggers

Corrective action should be initiated when any of the following occur:

- Vendor onboarding exceeds **5 business days**.
- Documentation completion falls below **100%**.
- Vendor approval rate drops below **95%**.
- Vendor performance score falls below **4.5 / 5**.
- On-time delivery rate falls below **95%**.
- SLA compliance falls below **95%**.
- Documentation accuracy falls below **99%**.
- High-risk vendors exceed **10%** of the vendor base.
- Corrective action completion falls below **100%**.
- Vendor retention falls below **90%**.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Dashboard results are used to:

- Identify process inefficiencies.
- Improve vendor onboarding procedures.
- Strengthen procurement decision-making.
- Enhance vendor performance monitoring.
- Reduce operational and supply chain risks.
- Support continual improvement in accordance with ISO 9001:2015.
