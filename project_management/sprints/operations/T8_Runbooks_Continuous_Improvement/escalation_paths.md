# Escalation Paths

## Purpose

Escalation paths define responsibility for resolving operational issues when standard procedures cannot resolve them.

---

## Escalation Levels

### Level 1

Operational Owner

Responsible for resolving routine operational issues.

---

### Level 2

Department Manager

Responsible for unresolved operational issues affecting workflow performance.

---

### Level 3

Chief Operating Officer (COO)

Responsible for critical operational failures affecting business operations.

---

## Escalation Triggers

Escalation occurs when:

- Critical workflow failure occurs
- SLA cannot be met
- Vendor issue remains unresolved
- Procurement process is blocked
- Supply chain disruption impacts operations

---

## Resolution

Resolved issues should be documented and incorporated into future operational improvements.
