# Review Cadence

## Purpose

Review Cadence establishes a recurring schedule for reviewing operational performance and identifying opportunities for improvement.

---

## Monthly Review

Review operational KPIs.

Review vendor performance.

Review procurement performance.

---

## Quarterly Review

Compare current performance against baseline metrics.

Review bottleneck improvements.

Review supply chain performance.

Review quality assurance metrics.

---

## Annual Review

Review operational documentation.

Review process ownership.

Review KPI targets.

Update operational standards where necessary.

---

## Deliverables

- KPI Review
- Improvement Backlog
- Updated Operational Documentation
