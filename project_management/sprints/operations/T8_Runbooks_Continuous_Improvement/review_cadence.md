# Review Cadence

**Sprint:** Operations Sprint — Quality Assurance, Supply Chains & Procurement  
**Owner:** COO / Operations Team  
**Review Cadence:** Monthly • Quarterly • Annually

---

# Purpose

The Review Cadence establishes a structured schedule for evaluating operational performance, monitoring key performance indicators (KPIs), and identifying opportunities for continuous improvement. Regular reviews help ensure Vynues' operational processes remain effective, efficient, and aligned with quality standards.

---

# Monthly Review

The Operations Team should review:

- KPI Dashboard (T7)
- Vendor onboarding performance
- Vendor performance
- Procurement performance
- Open defects and corrective actions
- Operational issues requiring follow-up

**Deliverables**

- Monthly KPI Review
- Corrective Action Updates
- Improvement Backlog

---

# Quarterly Review

The Operations Team should review:

- KPI trends compared to baseline performance
- Bottleneck improvements (T4)
- Supply chain performance (T3)
- Procurement processes (T5)
- Vendor management performance (T6)
- Quality Assurance performance (T1–T2)
- Operations Runbooks

**Deliverables**

- Quarterly Performance Review
- Process Improvement Recommendations
- Updated Runbooks (if needed)

---

# Annual Review

The Operations Team should review:

- Operational documentation
- Process ownership
- KPI targets
- Operational standards
- Continuous Improvement Process (T8)

Updates should be made where improvements are identified.

**Deliverables**

- Annual Operations Review
- Updated Operational Documentation
- Revised KPI Targets
- Continuous Improvement Plan
