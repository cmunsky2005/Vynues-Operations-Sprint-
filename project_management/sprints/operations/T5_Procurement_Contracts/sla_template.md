# PART 5 — RESPONSIBILITIES

### Vendor Responsibilities

The Vendor agrees to:

* Meet all agreed service levels.
* Deliver services by scheduled deadlines.
* Maintain regular communication with Vynues.
* Report delays or issues immediately.
* Correct service deficiencies within the agreed response times.

### Vynues Responsibilities

Vynues agrees to:

* Provide complete project requirements.
* Respond to vendor questions promptly.
* Review completed work within a reasonable timeframe.
* Communicate requested changes in writing.
* Provide timely payment according to the Procurement Contract.

---

# PART 6 — SERVICE FAILURE

A service failure occurs when the Vendor does not meet one or more service standards identified in this SLA.

Examples include:

* Missed delivery deadlines
* Failure to respond within required response times
* Failure to complete agreed services
* Repeated quality deficiencies

---

# PART 7 — CORRECTIVE ACTION

If service standards are not met:

1. Vynues documents the issue.
2. The Vendor is notified in writing.
3. The Vendor acknowledges the issue within the required response time.
4. The Vendor submits a corrective action plan.
5. Vynues reviews the completed corrective action.

Repeated service failures may result in remedies outlined in the associated Procurement Contract.

---

# PART 8 — PERFORMANCE REVIEW

Vendor performance will be reviewed based on:

* Quality of services
* Timeliness
* Communication
* Responsiveness
* Completion of deliverables
* Compliance with this SLA

Review results may be considered when selecting vendors for future projects.

---

# PART 9 — CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT

The Parties agree to periodically review this SLA to identify opportunities for improving service quality, communication, and operational efficiency. Any updates must be approved in writing by both parties.

---

# PART 10 — TERM OF AGREEMENT

This SLA becomes effective on the Effective Date and remains in effect for the duration of the associated Procurement Contract unless amended or terminated in writing by both parties.

---

# PART 11 — ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This SLA, together with the associated Procurement Contract and any approved written amendments, represents the complete agreement regarding service performance expectations.

### Contract law concepts incorporated

This SLA naturally incorporates several of the contract concepts from your scope:

* **Offer and Acceptance** — Both parties agree by signing the SLA.
* **Meeting of the Minds** — The SLA clearly defines shared performance expectations.
* **Consideration** — The SLA supports the exchange established in the Procurement Contract.
* **Substantial Performance** — Performance is measured through the service standards and KPIs.
* **Material Breach** — Repeated failure to meet service levels can trigger remedies under the Procurement Contract.
* **Integration Clause** — Part 11 states that the SLA and Procurement Contract together represent the complete agreement regarding service performance.

This keeps the legal principles at an appropriate internship level while focusing the SLA on its primary purpose: **measuring and managing vendor performance**.
