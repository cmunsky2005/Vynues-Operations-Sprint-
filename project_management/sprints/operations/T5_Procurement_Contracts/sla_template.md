------------------------------------------------------------
5. VENDOR RESPONSIBILITIES
------------------------------------------------------------

The vendor agrees to:

• Complete all services by the agreed deadlines.
• Maintain regular communication with Vynues.
• Notify Vynues immediately of any delays or issues.
• Deliver work that meets the quality standards in the Procurement Contract.
• Correct identified defects within the required timeframes.

------------------------------------------------------------
6. VYNUES RESPONSIBILITIES
------------------------------------------------------------

Vynues agrees to:

• Provide project information and requirements.
• Respond to vendor questions promptly.
• Review completed work within a reasonable timeframe.
• Make payments according to the Procurement Contract.
• Communicate any concerns or requested changes in writing.

------------------------------------------------------------
7. ISSUE REPORTING
------------------------------------------------------------

If a service issue occurs:

1. Vynues documents the issue.
2. The vendor is notified.
3. The vendor confirms receipt within the required response time.
4. The vendor provides a corrective action plan.
5. Vynues verifies that the issue has been resolved.

------------------------------------------------------------
8. SERVICE FAILURES
------------------------------------------------------------

If service standards are not met, Vynues may:

• Request corrective action.
• Require the vendor to correct deficiencies.
• Apply contract remedies outlined in the Procurement Contract.
• Review vendor performance before future projects.

------------------------------------------------------------
9. PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
------------------------------------------------------------

Vendor performance will be reviewed:

• At project completion
• After major milestones
• Whenever significant service issues occur

Performance reviews may consider:

• Timeliness
• Communication
• Quality of work
• Professionalism
• Overall contract performance

------------------------------------------------------------
10. CHANGES TO THIS SLA
------------------------------------------------------------

Any changes to this Service Level Agreement must be approved in writing by both Vynues and the vendor before taking effect.
