## What This Folder Is

This folder contains all the contract templates and service level agreements Vynues uses when hiring venues and vendors. It was built as part of the Operations Sprint (Task 5).

---

## What Each File Does

### `procurement_contracts/`
The formal agreement between Vynues and a vendor or venue. Both sides sign this. It covers who is involved, what is being provided, how much it costs, and what happens if something goes wrong.

### `sla_template/`
The performance standards document. It defines how well a vendor must perform — response times, quality levels, and what penalties apply when standards are not met. This is attached to the procurement contract.

### `vendor_contract_templates.md`
A simpler fill-in-the-blank version for the operations team to use when onboarding a new vendor. It references the procurement contract and SLA but is easier to work with day to day.

## Standards This Follows

| Standard | Why It Matters Here |
|---|---|
| **UCC Article 2** | Governs sale of goods — sets default rules for delivery, defects, and remedies |
| **SLA best practice** | Defines measurable performance so there is no argument about whether the vendor did their job |

---

## Key Terms to Know

| Term | 
|---|---|
| **SLA** | Service Level Agreement — the performance promises the vendor makes |
| **Defect severity (S1–S4)** | How serious a problem is, from critical (S1) to cosmetic (S4) |
| **Acceptance** | The moment Vynues officially confirms the vendor did their job |
| **Remedy** | What happens financially when something goes wrong |
| **Change order** | A written update to the contract when the scope or price changes |
