## PART 4 — QUALITY STANDARDS

### 4.1 Acceptance Criteria

Vynues will accept the deliverables when:

- All items listed in 3.2 have been delivered on time
- All services meet the quality standards described below
- No S1 or S2 defects are present at time of delivery

### 4.2 Defect Severity Levels

Severity levels follow the Vynues Defect Severity Taxonomy (T1) to ensure consistent classification between internal QA review and vendor contract remedies.

| Level | Name | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | Critical | Complete failure — event cannot proceed | Caterer does not show up |
| S2 | High | Significant failure — event is seriously impacted but has a workaround | Food served 2+ hours late |
| S3 | Medium | Partial failure — event is inconvenienced but not prevented from completing | One menu item missing |
| S4 | Low | Small issue — no real impact on event | Minor presentation issue |

### 4.3 Inspection and Acceptance Process

- Vynues representative inspects deliverables on [DATE] or upon delivery
- Any defects are logged and communicated to the vendor within [X] hours
- Vendor has [X] hours to remedy S1 and S2 defects
- Vynues issues written acceptance once all standards are met

### 4.4 Right to Reject

Vynues reserves the right to reject any deliverable that does not meet the acceptance criteria in 4.1. Rejection triggers the remedies in Part 6.
