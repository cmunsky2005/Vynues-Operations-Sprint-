# PART 2 — PURPOSE

The purpose of this Agreement is to establish an ongoing business relationship between Vynues and the Vendor. This Agreement defines the general responsibilities, expectations, and requirements that apply whenever the Vendor provides goods or services to Vynues.

Specific projects, pricing, deliverables, and timelines will be documented in separate Procurement Contracts or Purchase Orders.

---

# PART 3 — VENDOR ELIGIBILITY

The Vendor represents that it:

* Is legally authorized to conduct business.
* Holds all required licenses and permits.
* Maintains all certifications required to perform its services.
* Will comply with all applicable federal, state, and local laws.

The Vendor agrees to notify Vynues of any changes affecting its eligibility to provide services.

---

# PART 4 — VENDOR RESPONSIBILITIES

The Vendor agrees to:

* Perform services in a professional manner.
* Meet all agreed project deadlines.
* Maintain open communication throughout each project.
* Provide qualified personnel to perform services.
* Follow all applicable safety and regulatory requirements.
* Notify Vynues promptly of any delays or issues.
* Maintain accurate records related to services performed.

---

# PART 5 — VYNUES RESPONSIBILITIES

Vynues agrees to:

* Provide project requirements before work begins.
* Supply information necessary to complete requested services.
* Review completed work within a reasonable timeframe.
* Communicate requested changes in writing.
* Process payments according to the applicable Procurement Contract.

---

# PART 6 — QUALITY EXPECTATIONS

The Vendor agrees to:

* Meet Vynues' quality standards.
* Follow all requirements outlined in the applicable Procurement Contract.
* Comply with the associated Service Level Agreement (SLA).
* Correct identified deficiencies within agreed timeframes.

Vendor performance may be reviewed throughout the business relationship.

---

# PART 7 — INSURANCE

The Vendor agrees to maintain appropriate insurance coverage throughout the duration of this Agreement.

Examples include:

* General Liability Insurance
* Workers' Compensation (if applicable)
* Commercial Auto Insurance (if applicable)

Proof of insurance must be provided upon request.

---

# PART 8 — CONFIDENTIALITY

The Vendor agrees to keep confidential any business information, customer information, pricing information, or proprietary materials obtained while performing services for Vynues.

Confidential information may not be disclosed without prior written permission unless required by law.

---

# PART 9 — COMPLIANCE

The Vendor agrees to comply with:

* All applicable laws and regulations.
* Health and safety requirements.
* Equal employment laws.
* Vynues policies applicable to vendors.
* Ethical business practices.

---

# PART 10 — TERM OF AGREEMENT

This Agreement becomes effective on the Effective Date and remains in effect until terminated by either party in accordance with this Agreement.

---

# PART 11 — TERMINATION

Either party may terminate this Agreement by providing **30 days' written notice**.

Immediate termination may occur if the Vendor:

* Materially breaches this Agreement.
* Fails to maintain required licenses or insurance.
* Engages in fraudulent or unlawful activity.
* Refuses or is unable to perform agreed services.

Termination of this Agreement does not affect obligations under Procurement Contracts already in progress unless otherwise agreed in writing.
