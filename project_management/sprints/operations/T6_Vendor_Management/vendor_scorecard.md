# Vendor Performance Scorecard

## Purpose

The **Vendor Performance Scorecard** provides a standardized method for evaluating vendor performance after each completed project or service engagement. The scorecard helps Vynues measure vendor performance against established expectations, identify areas for improvement, and support future procurement decisions through objective performance data.

---

# Objectives

The objectives of the Vendor Performance Scorecard are to:

- Measure vendor performance using standardized evaluation criteria.
- Promote accountability and continuous improvement.
- Monitor compliance with Procurement Contracts and Service Level Agreements (SLAs).
- Support future vendor selection and procurement decisions.
- Identify high-performing vendors and vendors requiring corrective action.

---

# Performance Evaluation Process

## Step 1: Complete the Evaluation

At the conclusion of a project or service engagement, the project manager or Operations Team completes the Vendor Performance Scorecard.

### Evaluation Information

- Vendor Name
- Project or Event
- Evaluation Date
- Reviewer
- Contract Number (if applicable)

**Outcome:** Evaluation begins using standardized performance criteria.

---

## Step 2: Evaluate Performance

Each performance category is scored using a scale from **1 (Poor)** to **5 (Excellent)**.

| Performance Category | Weight | Description |
|----------------------|:------:|-------------|
| **Quality of Service** | 25% | Accuracy, completeness, and overall quality of products or services provided. |
| **On-Time Delivery** | 20% | Ability to meet agreed deadlines and delivery schedules. |
| **Communication** | 15% | Responsiveness, professionalism, and communication throughout the project. |
| **SLA Compliance** | 15% | Compliance with Service Level Agreement requirements and performance standards. |
| **Professionalism** | 10% | Professional conduct, collaboration, and customer service. |
| **Customer Satisfaction** | 10% | Overall satisfaction of internal stakeholders or clients. |
| **Documentation Accuracy** | 5% | Accuracy and completeness of invoices, reports, and required documentation. |

**Scoring Scale**

| Score | Rating |
|:----:|---------|
| **5** | Excellent |
| **4** | Good |
| **3** | Meets Expectations |
| **2** | Needs Improvement |
| **1** | Poor |

**Outcome:** Individual performance scores are assigned.

---

## Step 3: Calculate Overall Performance

Calculate the weighted score for each performance category and determine the vendor's overall performance rating.

| Overall Score | Vendor Classification |
|--------------:|----------------------|
| **4.5 – 5.0** | Preferred Vendor |
| **3.5 – 4.4** | Approved Vendor |
| **2.5 – 3.4** | Improvement Required |
| **Below 2.5** | Vendor Under Review |

**Outcome:** Vendor performance status is determined.

## Step 5: Follow-Up and Continuous Improvement

Performance results are used to support future procurement and vendor management decisions.

Based on the overall evaluation:

- **Preferred Vendors** may receive priority consideration for future projects.
- **Approved Vendors** remain eligible for future work.
- **Improvement Required** vendors may receive a corrective action plan and additional monitoring.
- **Vendors Under Review** may require management approval before future engagements or be removed from consideration.

Performance trends should be reviewed periodically to support continuous improvement.

**Outcome:** Vendor performance information supports long-term procurement decisions.

---

# Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| **Project Manager** | Completes the vendor performance evaluation after project completion. |
| **Operations Team** | Reviews scorecards, monitors vendor performance, and tracks improvement trends. |
| **Procurement Team** | Uses performance results during future vendor selection and contract decisions. |
| **Vendor** | Reviews feedback and implements corrective actions when necessary. |

---

# Expected Benefits

Implementing a standardized Vendor Performance Scorecard provides the following benefits:

- Consistent vendor evaluations
- Improved procurement decision-making
- Increased accountability
- Better vendor communication
- Identification of high-performing vendors
- Support for continuous improvement
- Stronger compliance with Procurement Contracts and SLAs
