# Vendor Risk Assessment Process

**Sprint:** Operations Sprint — Vendor Management (T6)
**Version:** 1.0
**Owner:** Operations Team
**Standards:** ISO 9001:2015 • SCOR Model • ISO 28000 • Procurement Best Practices

## Purpose

The Vendor Risk Assessment Process establishes a consistent approach for identifying, evaluating, monitoring, and managing risks associated with vendors throughout the procurement process. Effective risk management supports reliable operations, procurement activities, and supply chain performance.

## Objectives

The objectives of the Vendor Risk Assessment Process are to:

- Identify vendor-related risks.
- Evaluate operational risks associated with vendors.
- Support procurement decision-making.
- Reduce supply chain disruptions.
- Monitor vendor performance.
- Support continual improvement.

## Vendor Risk Assessment Process

### Step 1 – Collect Vendor Information

Relevant vendor information should be collected before procurement decisions are made.

Examples include:
- Vendor information
- Products or services provided
- Required documentation
- Contract information
- Regulatory information

### Step 2 – Evaluate Risk

Vendor information should be reviewed to identify potential operational risks.

Examples include:
- Operational risk
- Supply chain risk
- Contract compliance
- Service reliability
- Regulatory compliance

### Step 3 – Determine Risk

The Operations Team documents the results of the assessment and identifies any risks requiring additional monitoring or corrective action.

### Step 4 – Risk Mitigation

When risks are identified, appropriate mitigation activities should be implemented to reduce operational impact.

Examples include:
- Additional monitoring
- Corrective actions
- Documentation updates
- Procurement review
- Vendor communication

### Step 5 – Monitor and Review

Vendor performance and identified risks should be monitored and reviewed to determine whether additional actions are required.

Results should be incorporated into the Continuous Improvement Process.

## Vendor Risk Tiers

Following the Step 3 risk determination, each vendor is assigned to one of four risk tiers. The tier determines the monitoring frequency, documentation requirements, and escalation path applied to that vendor going forward. This tiering supports the Vendor Risk Classification metric tracked on the KPI Dashboard (T7).

| Tier | Criteria | Monitoring Requirement | Escalation Path |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Low** | Valid insurance and certifications on file; no SLA violations in the past 12 months; not a single-source or sole-provider vendor; stable financial standing | Annual documentation refresh | Level 1 — Operational Owner |
| **Medium** | Minor SLA violations (S3/S4 defects) in the past 12 months; certifications nearing expiration within 90 days; limited alternative vendors available | Semi-annual documentation refresh; performance reviewed at each Quarterly Review (T8) | Level 1 — Operational Owner, escalate to Level 2 if unresolved |
| **High** | One or more S2 (High) defects or SLA violations in the past 12 months; expired or lapsed certification within the past 12 months; sole-source vendor for a critical service (e.g., primary venue, exclusive caterer) | Quarterly documentation and insurance re-verification; performance reviewed monthly | Level 2 — Department Manager |
| **Critical** | One or more S1 (Critical) defects in the past 12 months; current lapse in required insurance or licensing; active contract dispute; financial instability affecting ability to deliver | Monthly re-verification; corrective action plan required before further project assignment | Level 3 — Chief Operating Officer (COO) |

**Tier Review**

- A vendor's tier is reassessed at each Vendor Performance Scorecard evaluation (T6) and whenever a new defect or SLA violation is logged against them.
- A vendor may move up a tier (improve) after two consecutive Preferred or Approved Vendor scorecard ratings with no new violations.
- A vendor may move down a tier (worsen) immediately upon an S1 or S2 defect, a lapsed certification, or an unresolved escalation.
- Critical-tier vendors require COO sign-off before being assigned to any new project.

## Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Operations Team | Conduct vendor risk assessments and document findings. |
| Procurement Team | Use assessment results during procurement activities. |
| Management | Review identified operational risks and support corrective actions. |
| Vendor | Provide requested documentation and cooperate during the assessment process. |

The Vendor Risk Assessment Process supports:

- Improved procurement decisions
- Reduced operational risk
- Improved supply chain performance
- Better vendor management
- Support for continual improvement
